## Research question

**Can a minimal panel of circulating inflammatory proteins improve the prediction of incident frontotemporal dementia (FTD) beyond demographics and established blood biomarkers?**

## The idea in one paragraph

The plan is to use UK Biobank baseline Olink plasma proteomics and compare three models: age and sex alone, then adding NfL, then adding a small set of inflammatory proteins. The main question is whether the third model predicts better than the second. See [Planned analysis](/docs/planned-analysis) for the detail, and [Where things stand](/docs/where-things-stand) for what is and is not done.
