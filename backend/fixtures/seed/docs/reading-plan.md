## What I am trying to find out

Group the reading into the questions below. Each matches a **review section** on the paper form, so papers sort themselves as you add them.

- [ ] **Peripheral inflammation in FTD.** Is there evidence of a blood inflammatory signal in people with FTD?
- [ ] **Before symptoms start.** Is any such signal present before onset (for example in people who carry a causative gene variant)?
- [ ] **Beyond existing blood markers.** Does it add anything over markers such as NfL?
- [ ] **Population and methods.** What study designs and data (such as large cohorts) could test this, and how?
- [ ] **Gaps.** What has not been done?
