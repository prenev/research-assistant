This is your personal research notebook. Everything is done from the browser: there is nothing to edit in code or files.

## Getting around

| Section | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Docs** | Planning pages like this one |
| **Papers** | Your reading list and what each paper found |
| **Proteins** | The proteins you are considering, with background and evidence |
| **Visualise** | Charts of the evidence (coming) |
| **Pipeline** | The steps of the analysis and their status |
| **Decisions** | What you decided, why, and whether it was prespecified |
| **Log** | Dated notes, like a research diary |

Press **Ctrl K** (or **⌘ K**) to search everything.

## Editing

1. Click the **pencil** in the top bar to turn on edit mode.
2. On a docs page, choose **Edit content**. It autosaves a draft, and **Save** publishes it.
3. Use **＋** for quick adds: a paper, protein, finding, log post, docs page or decision.
4. Every item has a **History** button to compare versions and restore an old one.
5. Deleting moves things to the **Trash**, where you can restore them for 30 days.

In the editor, type `/` to insert headings, lists, tables, code, callout boxes and the embeds below.

## Adding papers

Choose **＋ → Add paper** and paste a DOI to fill in the details, or import a BibTeX or Zotero export. Then use **Add finding** to record what the paper says about a protein.

## Protein background

On the **Proteins** page, tap **ⓘ Info** to read about a protein, with images, without leaving the site.

## Embeds

| Shortcode | Shows |
|---|---|
| `{{cite:1}}` or `{{cite:1,2}}` | A numbered reference such as [1], linking to the paper |
| `{{paper:slug}}` | A paper chip with a hover card |
| `{{protein:slug}}` | A protein chip |
| `{{viz:evidence-matrix population=general_population}}` | A chart (coming) |

## Keep a backup

**Settings → Download full backup** saves everything as one file. Do it now and then.
